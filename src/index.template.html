How the seal works

THE SEAL

A fingerprint of your text

SHA-256 turns the text into 64 characters. Change one letter, comma or line break and the seal changes completely. Nobody can work backwards from the seal to the text.

THE KEY

Stops anyone guessing

“Denmark wins” is easy to guess and check. A random secret key is sealed with your text, so nobody can test guesses against your seal before you reveal it.

THE TIME

Comes from where you publish

Your device's clock proves nothing. A dated post, email or message with the seal in it does. Nothing is stored anywhere, so the proof is yours.

Check it without this page

printf 'kvantix-seal-v1\n%s\n%s' "$KEY" "$TEXT" | sha256sum
python3 verify.py check receipt.txt --seal <published seal>
node verify.mjs check receipt.txt --seal <published seal>

The text is compared after three normalisation steps: line breaks become \n, Unicode NFC, surrounding whitespace removed. See SPEC.md.

What a seal can't do

It proves what you wrote, not that it was your only prediction. Sealing ten guesses and revealing the one that came true proves nothing. A real track record shows every call, including the misses. That is the rule behind every forward test Kvantix runs.
